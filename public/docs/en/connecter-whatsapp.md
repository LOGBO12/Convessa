# Connect Your WhatsApp Account

To send messages via CONVESSA, you need to connect your personal or business WhatsApp number to your account. This number becomes the sender for all your messages.

---

## Connection Steps

1. From your CONVESSA dashboard, click on **Sessions** in the sidebar menu
2. Click the **Connect a Number** button
3. A **unique QR Code** will appear on the screen (valid for 60 seconds)
4. Open WhatsApp on your phone and access **Linked devices**
5. Scan the displayed QR code

**On Android**: Menu (3 dots) > Linked devices > Link a device  
**On iPhone**: Settings > Linked devices > Link a device

---

## After Connection

Once the QR code is scanned, your account is connected and your API key will be available in the Sessions tab. You can then start sending messages via the API.

---

## Reconnecting

If your connection is interrupted, return to the **Sessions** tab, click **Reconnect** and scan the QR code again. Your API key remains unchanged.

---

## Best Practices

- Keep your smartphone connected to the internet (Wi-Fi or 4G/5G)
- On Android, disable battery optimization for WhatsApp
- Always manage the connection from your CONVESSA dashboard
